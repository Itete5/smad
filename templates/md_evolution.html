⬡ Scope / Honesty Note
This uses a generic Lennard-Jones pair potential (σ scaled from covalent radii, ε uniform) in reduced units — the same class of model used for generic glass-forming studies (e.g. Kob-Andersen). It is not a validated force field (no Tersoff/SW/ReaxFF) and temperatures are reduced-unit T*, not calibrated Kelvin. Treat the result as illustrative of the melt-quench procedure, not a quantitatively accurate model of a specific real material.
